MLB Schedule — Help
Spoiler-free by default. You control what you see.
Spoilers

Scores are hidden by default. Turn on Show scores in Filters whenever you're ready to see them. Completed games show "Final" until you toggle scores on.

Mark a game as watched with the ○ / ✓ button on any Final card. Use Hide watched to clean up your list.

Schedule view

Games are grouped by date. MLB plays nearly every day during the regular season (April–September), so you'll typically see a large slate of games per day.

During the off-season (November–February), a placeholder card is shown until Spring Training data becomes available.

Starting pitchers

When available, probable starting pitchers are shown below the team names on each game card — including ERA if the API provides it. Live games may show the current pitcher instead.

Doubleheaders

When two teams play twice in a day, each game card shows a Game 1 or Game 2 badge so you can tell them apart.

League & Division filters

Filter by American League (AL) or National League (NL), then narrow by Division (East, Central, West). You can combine them — e.g. AL + East shows only AL East games.

Note: interleague games will appear for both leagues.

My Team

Open Filters → Favorite teams, star your team, then tap ☆ My Team to set it. Your team's colors take over the app, your games are highlighted, and the team logo appears in the header.

Teams view & rosters

Switch to Teams to browse all 30 clubs organized by league and division. Tap any team to open its roster. Tap any player for their profile and recent game stats.

You can also open rosters from the Schedule view by tapping the Roster pill on any game card.

Saving players

Tap ★ on any player in a roster to save them. Open Filters → Players to see your saved players list and jump straight to their profiles.

Saving games

Tap ★ on any game card to bookmark it. Use Filters → Saved to see only your bookmarked games.

Archive

Games older than 14 days roll into the archive. Enable Show archive in Filters to see the full history.

Time zones

All times default to your device's local time. Change the time zone in Filters → the dropdown at the bottom of the filter panel.

Data & updates

Schedule data comes from the ESPN public API and refreshes automatically every 10 minutes. Tap Refresh in the header to force an immediate update. During game days the scraper keeps live scores and inning info current.
